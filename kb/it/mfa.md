# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

## Policy
MFA is mandatory for every NovaTech account. The approved method is the **Okta Verify** app (push or six-digit code). SMS codes are allowed only as a temporary backup for up to 7 days.

## First-time setup
1. Install Okta Verify from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store on your phone.
2. On your laptop, sign in at https://novatech.okta.com. You will be prompted to set up MFA.
3. Choose **Okta Verify**, then scan the QR code shown on screen with the app.
4. Approve the test push to finish enrolment.

## Changing to a new phone
Before you wipe your old phone, sign in at https://novatech.okta.com, go to **Settings > Security Methods** and click **Set up another** for Okta Verify on the new phone. Then remove the old device.

## Lost or stolen phone
Call the IT Service Desk on extension 2020 immediately. IT will remove the lost device and issue a one-time temporary access code valid for 24 hours so that you can enrol a new phone. If the phone also had company email, IT will remotely wipe the work profile.

## Hardware security keys
Engineers with production access and Finance staff must additionally register a YubiKey. Request one through the IT Service Catalog under **Hardware > Security Key**; it is shipped within 3 working days.
