# Office Wi-Fi & Guest Wi-Fi Access

## Employee Wi-Fi (NovaTech-Corp)
Company laptops connect automatically to **NovaTech-Corp** using a device certificate; no password is needed. If your laptop does not connect, restart it while plugged into a LAN port on your desk, or use the **NovaTech-Setup** network and follow the on-screen enrolment.

Personal phones and tablets must use **NovaTech-BYOD**. Sign in with your SSO credentials; you can register up to 2 personal devices.

## Guest Wi-Fi (NovaTech-Guest)
Visitors, interview candidates and vendors use **NovaTech-Guest**.

1. The host employee creates a guest pass at https://wifi.novatech.com/guest.
2. Enter the visitor's name, email or mobile number, and visit duration (maximum 7 days).
3. The visitor receives a voucher code by email or text message.
4. The visitor connects to NovaTech-Guest and enters the voucher code.

Guest Wi-Fi is internet-only and isolated from internal systems. Bandwidth is limited to 10 Mbps per device. For events with more than 20 guests, request a bulk voucher through the IT Service Catalog at least 3 working days in advance.

## Office locations
The Minneapolis HQ (North Loop), the Chicago office and the Denver office all use the same network names.
