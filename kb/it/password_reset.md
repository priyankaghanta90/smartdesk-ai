# Password Reset & Account Lockout

## Self-service password reset
NovaTech uses a single sign-on (SSO) account for email, Slack, Jira, the HR portal (PeopleHub) and the VPN. To reset your SSO password yourself:

1. Go to the Self-Service Password Portal at https://passwords.novatech.com.
2. Click **Forgot Password** and enter your employee ID (format `NT` followed by five digits, e.g. NT10432).
3. Choose to receive a one-time code on your registered personal email or on the Okta Verify app.
4. Enter the code and set a new password.

The new password takes effect on all SSO apps within five minutes. On your laptop, lock and unlock the screen while connected to the office network or VPN so Windows/macOS picks up the new password.

## Password rules
- Minimum 12 characters, with at least one upper-case letter, one number and one symbol.
- Must not reuse any of your last 5 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. You get email reminders 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry.

## Account lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes and then unlocks automatically. If you need access sooner, or you are locked out and cannot receive the one-time code, call the IT Service Desk on extension 2020 (or +1 612-555-2020 from outside) between 7 AM and 9 PM CT, Monday to Saturday. The agent will verify your identity with your employee ID and manager's name before unlocking.

## Forgotten laptop (BitLocker / FileVault) recovery key
If your laptop asks for a BitLocker or FileVault recovery key, do not reinstall anything. Call extension 2020; IT can read the recovery key to you after identity verification.
