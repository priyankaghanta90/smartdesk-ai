# Laptop & Device Policy

## Standard devices
- **Engineering and Data teams**: Apple MacBook Pro 14" (M-series, 32 GB RAM) or Lenovo ThinkPad T14s with Ubuntu on request.
- **All other teams**: Lenovo ThinkPad E14 or Apple MacBook Air 13" (16 GB RAM).
- Each employee also receives a 24" external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset for the office or home desk.

## Refresh cycle
Laptops are replaced every **3 years**. IT emails you 30 days before your refresh date with a booking link to swap devices. Early replacement needs manager approval and a documented performance or hardware issue.

## Damage, loss and theft
- Report loss or theft within 24 hours by calling extension 2020, and file a police report for theft and share the report number with IT.
- Accidental damage is covered by insurance for the first incident; the second incident in a refresh cycle may be charged at up to $500.
- Never attempt your own hardware repair or open the laptop casing.

## Loaner laptops
While your laptop is being repaired, collect a loaner from the IT desk on the 3rd floor at the Minneapolis HQ (or the IT room in the Chicago and Denver offices). Loaners are available for up to 14 days.

## Returning devices
On your last working day, return the laptop, charger and any accessories to IT. Unreturned equipment is recovered from the final settlement.
