# Email on Mobile Devices

## Supported apps
NovaTech email runs on Google Workspace. On mobile you must use the official **Gmail** app (Android or iOS). The built-in iOS Mail app and third-party clients such as Outlook mobile are blocked by policy.

## Android setup
1. Install the **Android Device Policy** app from the Play Store.
2. Open Settings > Accounts > Add account > Google and sign in with your NovaTech email.
3. Approve the Okta Verify prompt.
4. Accept the work-profile setup. Work apps (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) appear with a briefcase badge.

## iOS setup
1. Install **Google Device Policy** and **Gmail** from the App Store.
2. Open Gmail, tap **Add account > Google**, and sign in with your NovaTech email.
3. Approve the Okta Verify prompt and install the management profile when asked.

## Security requirements on the phone
- Screen lock with a PIN of at least 6 digits or biometrics.
- OS version no more than two major versions behind the latest.
- Rooted or jail-broken phones are blocked automatically.

## Calendar and Slack
Google Calendar and Slack follow the same sign-in. If notifications stop, sign out of the app, remove the work profile account, and add it again.

## Leaving NovaTech or losing the phone
On your last day, or if the phone is lost, IT removes the work profile remotely. Only work data is erased; your personal photos and apps stay untouched.
