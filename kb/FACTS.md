## Work arrangements
- Hybrid: 3 office days (anchor days Tue/Wed/Thu), eligible after the 90-day introductory period.
- Core hours 10 AM to 4 PM CT. Outside the US: not allowed without HR + Legal approval. Another US state for more than 2 weeks: HR approval (state payroll tax).
- Home-office allowance: $500 one-time. Internet: up to $50/month.

## Pay and compensation
- Payday: every other Friday by direct deposit (26 paychecks a year); the Thursday before if Friday is a bank holiday.
- Payroll cut-off: 5 PM CT on the Monday of payday week. Off-cycle corrections paid within 3 business days.
- Bonus target: 10% of base salary (15% for managers and above), paid in March. Merit increases effective 1 April.
- 401(k) with Fidelity: auto-enrolled at 3%; NovaTech matches 100% of the first 4% plus 50% of the next 2% (5% max); match vests immediately.
- Taxes withheld: federal income tax (W-4), Minnesota income tax (W-4MN), Social Security 6.2%, Medicare 1.45%.
- W-2 on PeopleHub by 31 January.
- Stock options: 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff (25% at 12 months, then 6.25% quarterly), managed in Carta. Exercise window after leaving: 90 days.

## Benefits
- Medical through Northstar Health: PPO or HDHP with HSA. NovaTech pays 90% of employee premiums and 75% for dependants.
- HSA employer contribution: $750/yr (individual), $1,500/yr (family). Dental and vision included.
- Enrol within 30 days of start; otherwise open enrollment in November or within 30 days of a qualifying life event.
- Dependants: spouse or domestic partner, children up to age 26.
- Life insurance and AD&D: 2x base salary each, free. Short- and long-term disability: free.
- Fitness reimbursement: $50/month.
