# Work-From-Home (Hybrid) Policy

## Hybrid model
NovaTech follows a hybrid model: employees work from the office **3 days a week** (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are the team "anchor days") and may work from home on the other 2 working days. Teams may agree on different anchor days with their department head.

## Eligibility
- Employees who have completed their 90-day introductory period are eligible for hybrid working.
- During the introductory period, employees work from the office 5 days a week unless their manager approves otherwise.
- Roles that need on-site presence (Facilities, IT desk support, Reception) are exempt.

## Fully remote requests
Fully remote work (more than 2 days a week at home) for longer than 2 weeks needs approval from both the reporting manager and the HR Business Partner. Requests are raised on PeopleHub under **Requests > Remote Work** and are reviewed every 6 months.
- Working from another US state for more than 2 weeks needs HR approval in advance, because NovaTech must withhold that state's payroll taxes.
- Working from outside the United States is not allowed without prior approval from HR and Legal because of tax, immigration and data-protection rules.

## Expectations while working from home
- Be available on Slack and phone during core hours of 10 AM to 4 PM CT.
- Use a secure home network; connect to the VPN for internal systems.
- Do not work from public places when handling customer data.
- Keep your camera on for team meetings unless your bandwidth is poor.

## Home-office allowance
Employees receive a one-time home-office allowance of **$500** after the introductory period for a chair, desk or other ergonomic items, plus a monthly internet reimbursement of up to **$50**. Claim both through the reimbursement process on PeopleHub with receipts.
