# Anti-Harassment & Equal Employment Opportunity Policy

## Scope
NovaTech has zero tolerance for harassment, bullying and discrimination. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, creed, religion, national origin, sex, pregnancy, gender identity, sexual orientation, marital or familial status, disability, age, veteran status, public assistance status or any other characteristic protected by federal law (Title VII, the ADA and the ADEA) or the Minnesota Human Rights Act. The policy covers employees, interns, contractors and visitors, both at the workplace and at work-related events, travel and online communication.

## What counts as sexual harassment
Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal, physical or visual conduct of a sexual nature, when submitting to it is made a condition of employment or used for employment decisions (quid pro quo), or when it creates an intimidating, hostile or offensive work environment. Examples include unwanted touching, sexual jokes or comments, and sharing sexually explicit images.

## How to report harassment
1. Report to HR Employee Relations at er@novatech.com, to your HR Business Partner, or to any manager. Managers must pass every report to Employee Relations within 1 business day.
2. You can also report anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at https://ethics.novatech.com or 1-888-555-0167.
3. There is no internal deadline, but please report as soon as possible so the facts can be investigated.
4. You also have the right to file a charge with the US Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) within 300 days, or with the Minnesota Department of Human Rights within 1 year of the incident.

## Investigation
Employee Relations acknowledges a report within **2 business days** and assigns a trained investigator who is independent of the people involved. Investigations are normally completed within **30 days**, and both parties are told the outcome in writing.

## Confidentiality and protection
Reports are shared only with people who need to know to investigate and act. Interim measures, such as a change of reporting line or paid leave, may be offered during the investigation. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith or takes part in an investigation is itself a serious violation.

## Training
All employees must complete the online anti-harassment training within 30 days of joining and once every year after that. People managers complete an additional module on handling reports.
