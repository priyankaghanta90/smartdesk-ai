# Performance Review Cycle

## Cycle overview
NovaTech runs two review cycles each year:
- **Mid-year check-in** in July: a development conversation with no rating.
- **Annual review** in January: a formal rating that drives merit increases, bonuses and promotions.

Employees who joined after 1 October are not rated in that year's annual review; they receive their first rating in the next cycle.

## Goals
Set 3 to 5 goals (OKRs) on PeopleHub by 31 January, agreed with your manager. Goals can be updated at the mid-year check-in.

## Annual review steps
1. **Self-assessment** (1–10 January): rate yourself and give examples against each goal.
2. **Peer feedback** (by 15 January): request feedback from 3 to 5 colleagues on PeopleHub.
3. **Manager assessment** (by 20 January).
4. **Calibration** (late January): department leaders calibrate ratings across teams.
5. **Results conversation** (February): your manager shares your final rating.
6. **Merit increases** take effect from 1 April and appear in the first paycheck after that date.

## Rating scale
1 — Does not meet expectations, 2 — Partially meets, 3 — Meets expectations, 4 — Exceeds expectations, 5 — Outstanding.

## Promotions
Promotions are considered once a year in the annual cycle. You need a rating of 4 or above in the current cycle and at least 18 months in your current level.

## Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
An employee rated 1 is placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan with clear goals and biweekly check-ins with the manager and HR.

## Disagreeing with a rating
You can raise a review request with your HR Business Partner within 15 days of the results conversation. A skip-level manager and HR review the case and respond within 15 working days.
