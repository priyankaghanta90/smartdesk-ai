# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## How to submit a reimbursement
1. Log in to PeopleHub (https://peoplehub.novatech.com) and open **Expenses > New Claim**.
2. Choose the expense category, enter the amount, date and business purpose.
3. Upload a clear photo or PDF of each receipt. An itemized receipt is required for any single item above $75.
4. Submit. Your manager approves the claim, then Finance verifies it.

Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days are rejected.

## Payment timeline
Claims approved by Finance by 5 PM CT on the Monday of a payday week are reimbursed by direct deposit on that Friday's payday. Claims approved later are paid on the following payday. Reimbursements are shown separately from your salary and are not taxed.

## Eligible expenses and limits
| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Internet (WFH) | $50 per month |
| Client meals | $75 per person, with attendee names |
| Local travel (taxi/rideshare/transit) for work | Actuals, with trip receipts |
| Personal car for work trips | IRS standard mileage rate |
| Domestic travel | Economy air or rail, booked via the travel desk |
| Hotel (high-cost cities: New York, San Francisco, Boston, Chicago, Washington DC, Seattle) | $250 per night |
| Hotel (other cities) | $175 per night |
| Meals per diem on travel | $75 per day |
| Learning & certifications | Up to $2,500 per year, pre-approved by manager |
| Mobile phone bill | $50 per month for customer-facing roles |

## Not reimbursable
Alcohol (except at approved client meals), personal entertainment, traffic or speeding tickets, upgrades to first or business class, and expenses without receipts.

## International travel
International travel must be pre-approved by the department head and booked only through the corporate travel desk (travel@novatech.com). Per-diem rates are country-specific and shared with the travel itinerary.
