# Leave Policy

## Leave year and entitlement
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Full-time employees receive:

| Leave type | Amount per year | Accrual |
|---|---|---|
| Paid time off (PTO) | 15 days | Accrues 1.25 days per month |
| Sick and safe time (ESST) | Up to 48 hours (6 days) | Accrues 1 hour for every 30 hours worked |
| Company holidays | 10 fixed + 2 floating | Published each December |

Employees who join mid-year accrue PTO from their start date.

## Paid time off (PTO)
PTO is for vacations, personal errands and any other time away. PTO starts accruing on your first day and can be used after your 90-day introductory period. Apply on PeopleHub at least 2 business days in advance for a single day, and at least 2 weeks in advance for 3 or more days. You can carry over up to **5 days** of unused PTO into the next year; anything above 5 days is forfeited on 31 December. Unused accrued PTO is paid out at your base rate when you leave NovaTech.

## Sick and safe time
NovaTech follows the Minnesota Earned Sick and Safe Time (ESST) law. You accrue 1 hour of sick and safe time for every 30 hours worked, up to **48 hours (6 days)** per year. You can use it for your own illness or medical care, to care for a family member, for safety reasons related to domestic abuse, sexual assault or stalking, or when a school or care facility closes because of weather or a public emergency.
- Tell your manager as soon as possible; for foreseeable absences, give 7 days' notice.
- Documentation can be requested only for absences of more than **3 consecutive scheduled workdays**.
- Unused sick and safe time **carries over** to the next year, with the total balance capped at 80 hours. It is **not** paid out when you leave.

## Parental and family leave
- **Parental leave**: 12 weeks at full pay for any new parent (birth, adoption or foster placement), taken within 12 months of the child's arrival, in one block or in up to 3 blocks of at least 2 weeks.
- **Pregnancy recovery**: birthing parents receive up to 6 additional weeks at full pay.
- **Minnesota Paid Leave**: since 1 January 2026 the state program provides up to 12 weeks of medical leave and up to 12 weeks of family leave, with a maximum of 20 weeks combined per benefit year. NovaTech pays the full premium. NovaTech parental leave runs at the same time as Minnesota Paid Leave and FMLA, and NovaTech tops up the state benefit to 100% of your base pay.
- **FMLA**: up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave per year after 12 months and 1,250 hours of service.
- **Bereavement leave**: 5 days for the loss of an immediate family member, 2 days for extended family.
- **Jury duty**: full pay for up to 10 days per year.

## Working on weekends and holidays
If your manager asks you to work on a weekend or a company holiday, salaried (exempt) employees earn one substitute day off per full day worked. Apply for it on PeopleHub within 7 days of working and use it within 60 days, otherwise it lapses. Hourly (non-exempt) employees are paid overtime at 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek instead.

## Approval process
All leave is applied for and approved on PeopleHub (https://peoplehub.novatech.com). Your reporting manager approves; if the manager does not act within 3 business days, the request auto-escalates to the skip-level manager. Absence without approval is unpaid, except sick and safe time used as described above.

## Holidays
NovaTech observes 10 fixed holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. You also get 2 floating holidays per year to use for any cultural or religious occasion. The calendar for the next year is published each December.
