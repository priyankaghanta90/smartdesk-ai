# Code of Conduct

## Core principles
Every NovaTech employee is expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues and customers with respect, protect company and customer data, and follow the law. The Code applies in the office, at client sites, at off-sites and in online spaces such as Slack.

## Conflicts of interest
You must disclose any situation where your personal interests could conflict with NovaTech's, for example a family member working at a vendor, or a side business. Declare it on PeopleHub under **Compliance > Conflict of Interest** within 15 days of it arising. Outside employment or freelancing in the software industry requires written approval from HR.

## Gifts and hospitality
You may accept gifts from business partners worth up to **$100** in total per year per partner. Anything above that must be declined or declared to Compliance. Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards) must never be accepted.

## Confidentiality and data protection
Customer data may only be accessed for a valid business reason. Never share confidential information on personal email, personal cloud storage or public AI tools. Report any suspected data leak to security@novatech.com immediately.

## Social media
Do not post confidential information or speak on behalf of NovaTech without approval from the Communications team. Personal opinions should be clearly marked as your own.

## Reporting concerns (whistleblowing)
Report violations to your manager, your HR Business Partner, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at https://ethics.novatech.com or 1-888-555-0167. NovaTech prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Consequences
Breaches of the Code may lead to disciplinary action, from a written warning up to termination of employment, depending on severity.
