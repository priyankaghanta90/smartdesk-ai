# kb/payroll/

## Why this folder exists
Pay and compensation policies, answered by the **HR specialist**. The folder name sets the domain of every chunk inside it, so retrieval can filter to the right specialist.

| `payday.md` | Payday Schedule & Pay Statements | When and how employees are paid, and pay corrections |
| `salary_structure.md` | Compensation & Pay Structure | Pay components and 401(k) |
| `tax_declaration.md` | Tax Withholding Forms & Year-End Tax Documents | W-4 and W-2 questions |
| `esop_vesting.md` | Stock Option Plan & Vesting | Stock options and the vesting schedule |
