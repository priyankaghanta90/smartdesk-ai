# Stock Option Plan & Vesting

## Who gets stock options
Stock options are granted to all full-time employees at Level L3 and above at hire, and as refresh grants based on performance in the annual cycle. Your grant agreement shows the number of options, the option type (Incentive Stock Options, ISOs, for most employees) and the **exercise (strike) price**.

## Vesting schedule
NovaTech options vest over **4 years with a 1-year cliff**:
- **0%** vests in the first 12 months.
- **25%** vests on your first grant anniversary (the cliff).
- The remaining **75%** vests in equal **quarterly** installments over the next 3 years (6.25% per quarter).
For example, with 4,000 options, 1,000 vest at 12 months and 250 vest every quarter after that until month 48.

## Exercising options
Once options vest, you can exercise them (buy the shares at the strike price) through **Carta**. For ISOs, exercising does not create regular income tax, but the spread between the fair market value and the strike price may trigger the Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT). For Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs), the spread is taxed as ordinary income and withheld through payroll. Talk to a tax advisor before exercising.

## Leaving NovaTech
- Unvested options are forfeited on your last day.
- Vested options can be exercised within **90 days** of your last day; after that they expire.
- If you are terminated for cause, all options, vested and unvested, are forfeited.

## Liquidity
NovaTech is a private company. Shares can be sold only during company-approved tender offers or secondary-sale events, which have happened roughly every 18 to 24 months. Participation rules are announced for each event.
