# Payday Schedule & Pay Statements

## When is payday?
NovaTech pays employees **every other Friday** by direct deposit, which is 26 paychecks a year. If a payday falls on a bank holiday, you are paid on the Thursday before. New hires are paid on the first payday after their first full pay period; if you start after the payroll cut-off, your first paycheck includes the extra days.

## Payroll cut-off
The payroll cut-off is **5 PM CT on the Monday** of each payday week. Changes made after the cut-off (direct-deposit updates, approved reimbursements, unpaid leave, new hires, timesheets for hourly employees) are processed on the following payday.

## Pay statements
Pay statements are published on PeopleHub under **Payroll > Pay Statements** on each payday. They show your gross pay, reimbursements, pre-tax deductions (401(k), medical, dental, vision, HSA/FSA), taxes withheld (federal income tax, Minnesota income tax, Social Security and Medicare) and net pay.

## Changing your bank account
Update your direct-deposit details on PeopleHub under **Profile > Direct Deposit** and upload a voided check or a bank letter. The account must be in your name. You can split your pay across up to 3 accounts. Changes are verified by Payroll within 3 business days and apply to the next payday if completed before the cut-off.

## Payroll queries
For pay mistakes or missing payments, email payroll@novatech.com with your employee ID and the pay date. Payroll responds within 2 business days. Off-cycle corrections are paid by direct deposit within **3 business days** of being confirmed.
