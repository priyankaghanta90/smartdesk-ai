# Tax Withholding Forms & Year-End Tax Documents

## Federal Form W-4
Form W-4 tells Payroll how much federal income tax to withhold from each paycheck. New hires complete it on PeopleHub under **Payroll > Tax Forms** before their first payday. If you do not submit a W-4, federal tax is withheld as if you were single with no adjustments.

## Minnesota Form W-4MN
Because NovaTech is based in Minnesota, Minnesota residents and employees working in Minnesota also complete **Form W-4MN** for state income-tax withholding, in the same PeopleHub section. Employees in the Chicago or Denver offices complete their state's form instead (Illinois IL-W-4 or Colorado DR 0004).

## Updating your withholding
You can update your W-4 or W-4MN on PeopleHub at any time, for example after marriage, a new child or a second job. Changes made before the payroll cut-off (5 PM CT on the Monday of payday week) apply to that Friday's paycheck. Use the IRS Tax Withholding Estimator to check that your withholding is right.

## Form W-2
Your **Form W-2** for the previous calendar year is published on PeopleHub by **31 January**. It shows your wages and the taxes withheld, and you need it to file your federal and state tax returns by the April deadline. Corrected W-2s (W-2c) are issued within 10 business days of a confirmed error.

## Working from another state
If you work from another US state for more than 2 weeks, tell HR in advance (see the Work-From-Home policy). Payroll may need to withhold that state's income tax, and you will receive a W-2 that shows wages for each state.
