# Compensation & Pay Structure

## Components of your compensation
Your offer letter lists your total compensation, which is made up of:

| Component | How it works |
|---|---|
| Base salary | Annual amount, paid in 26 biweekly paychecks |
| Annual performance bonus | Variable; target is 10% of base salary (15% for managers and above) |
| 401(k) employer match | Up to 5% of eligible pay (see below) |
| Stock options | 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff (see the Stock Option Plan policy) |
| Benefits | Medical, dental, vision, life and disability insurance, HSA contribution |

## 401(k) retirement plan
NovaTech's 401(k) plan is administered by **Fidelity**. You are auto-enrolled at a 3% pre-tax contribution from your first paycheck and can change the rate, or choose Roth contributions, at any time on the Fidelity site. NovaTech matches **100% of the first 4%** you contribute plus **50% of the next 2%**, a maximum match of 5% of eligible pay. The match vests immediately.

## Taxes and withholding
Each paycheck has federal income tax, Minnesota state income tax, Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%) withheld. Pre-tax deductions such as 401(k), medical premiums and HSA contributions lower your taxable pay. NovaTech pays the full Minnesota Paid Leave premium, so there is no employee deduction for it.

## Performance bonus
The annual bonus is paid with the first paycheck in **March**, based on your annual rating and company performance. You must be employed on the payout date to receive it. Employees who joined after 1 October receive a pro-rated bonus.

## Pre-tax benefit accounts
You can contribute to a Health Savings Account (HSA) if you are on the high-deductible medical plan, or to a healthcare or dependent-care Flexible Spending Account (FSA), up to the annual IRS limits. Elect them on PeopleHub under **Benefits** at hire, during open enrollment, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event. Unused healthcare FSA money above the IRS carryover limit is forfeited at year end.

## Salary revisions
Merit increases are decided after the January review cycle and take effect from **1 April**. Revision letters are published on PeopleHub in March.

## Confidentiality
Salary information is confidential. Discussing your own pay is your right, but sharing other employees' compensation data that you access through your job is a violation of the Code of Conduct.
