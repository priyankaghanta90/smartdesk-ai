# Benefits & Insurance Enrollment

## Medical, dental and vision insurance
NovaTech provides medical insurance through **Northstar Health**, with coverage starting on your first day.
- Plans: a **PPO** plan, or a **High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP)** with a Health Savings Account (HSA).
- NovaTech pays **90%** of the premium for employees and **75%** for covered dependants; your share is deducted pre-tax from each paycheck.
- With the HDHP, NovaTech contributes **$750 a year** to your HSA for individual coverage, or **$1,500** for family coverage.
- Dental and vision plans are included, with the same premium split.
- Covered dependants: your spouse or domestic partner, and children up to age 26.

## How to enroll in benefits
1. Log in to PeopleHub and open **Benefits > Enrollment**.
2. Choose your medical, dental and vision plans, and add your dependants with their date of birth and relationship.
3. Submit within **30 days** of your start date. After 30 days, you can change elections only during open enrollment in November or within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, adoption or loss of other coverage).
4. Your insurance ID cards are mailed by Northstar Health within 7 business days; digital cards are available in the Northstar app.

## Other insurance
- **Basic life insurance**: 2 times your annual base salary, at no cost to you.
- **AD&D insurance**: 2 times your annual base salary, at no cost to you.
- **Short-term and long-term disability insurance**: at no cost to you. Short-term disability works alongside Minnesota Paid Leave.
- Voluntary supplemental life insurance can be bought for yourself, your spouse or partner and children.

## Wellness benefits
- Free annual preventive check-ups under every medical plan.
- Free, confidential counseling through the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) at 1-888-555-0143, 24/7, including up to 6 free sessions per issue.
- Fitness reimbursement of up to $50 per month.

## Retirement benefits
NovaTech offers a 401(k) plan with Fidelity. You are auto-enrolled at 3%, and NovaTech matches 100% of the first 4% plus 50% of the next 2% you contribute. See the Compensation & Pay Structure policy for details.
