# Day-One Checklist for New Hires

## Before your first day
- Your HR onboarding partner emails you a welcome kit 5 days before your start date, with your employee ID, start time and office address.
- Complete your new-hire paperwork on PeopleHub (via the pre-boarding link): Section 1 of **Form I-9**, Form W-4, Form W-4MN (or your state's form), direct-deposit details and emergency contacts.
- Complete the background-check consent form. The check is done by our partner agency and usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

## Your first day
1. Report to the reception at **9:30 AM** at the Minneapolis HQ (North Loop, 5th floor), or at the office in your offer letter (Chicago or Denver).
2. Bring original identity documents for your **Form I-9** (for example a US passport, or a driver's license plus a Social Security card). You must show them by your third business day.
3. Collect your access badge from the Facilities desk.
4. Attend the **NovaTech Welcome session** (10:00 AM to 1:00 PM) covering company history, values, policies and the benefits overview.
5. Collect your laptop from the IT desk (see the IT Equipment Allocation guide) and set up your SSO password and Okta Verify MFA.
6. Lunch with your team and your onboarding buddy.
7. Meet your manager to review your 30-60-90 day plan.

## First week
- Complete the mandatory e-learning modules on the Learning portal: Code of Conduct, Information Security, and Anti-Harassment (all within 30 days, but we recommend the first week).
- Enroll in medical, dental and vision benefits and add dependants within 30 days of your start date.
- Check your 401(k) contribution rate on the Fidelity site (you are auto-enrolled at 3%).
- Set up your PeopleHub profile, Slack profile photo and calendar working hours.
