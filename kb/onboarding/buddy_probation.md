# Buddy Program & Introductory Period

## Buddy program
Every new hire is paired with an **onboarding buddy**: a colleague from the same or a neighboring team, who is not their manager. Your buddy's name is in your welcome email.
- The buddy meets you on day one and has a weekly 30-minute coffee chat for the first 6 weeks.
- They help with informal questions: tools, team rituals, who to ask for what.
- After 6 weeks you will receive a short survey on PeopleHub about the buddy experience.
Employees with at least 1 year at NovaTech can volunteer to be buddies on PeopleHub under **Programs > Buddy**; buddies receive 20 recognition points per new hire.

## Introductory period
- The introductory period is **90 days** from the start date for all full-time employees.
- Your manager holds check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days, with a final review at day 90.
- On successful completion you receive a confirmation note on PeopleHub.
- The introductory period can be extended once, by up to 30 days, if goals are not met. You will be informed in writing with the reasons.

## During the introductory period
- You accrue PTO and sick and safe time from day one; PTO can be used after 90 days, while sick and safe time can be used as soon as it is accrued.
- You are not eligible for hybrid work during the introductory period (see the WFH policy), internal job transfers or the employee referral bonus.
- Benefits (medical, dental, vision, 401(k)) start on day one and are not affected by the introductory period.

## Resigning and notice
Employment at NovaTech is **at-will**, which means either you or NovaTech can end it at any time, during or after the introductory period. NovaTech asks employees to give **2 weeks' notice** when resigning (**4 weeks** for managers and above), submitted on PeopleHub under **Requests > Resignation**. Your final paycheck, including unused accrued PTO, is paid on the next regular payday.
