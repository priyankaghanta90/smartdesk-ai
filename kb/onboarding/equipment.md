# IT Equipment Allocation for New Hires

## When you get your laptop
Your laptop is pre-configured and handed over at the IT desk **on your first day**, after the Welcome session (around 2:00 PM). Remote new hires receive the laptop by courier **2 working days before** their start date at the address given in the pre-boarding form; please be available to sign for the delivery.

## What you receive
- Laptop according to your role (see the Laptop & Device Policy).
- Charger, a laptop bag, a headset with microphone.
- An external monitor, keyboard and mouse at your assigned office desk. Remote employees can request these for home through the IT Service Catalog under **Hardware > Home Desk Kit**.

## First login
1. Turn on the laptop and connect to the **NovaTech-Setup** Wi-Fi network (or the LAN cable at the IT desk).
2. Sign in with the temporary password in your welcome email.
3. You will be asked to set a new SSO password and enrol Okta Verify MFA.
4. Device management will install your standard apps; this takes about 30 minutes.

## Accounts created for you
Email (Google Workspace), Slack, Jira, Confluence, PeopleHub and the Learning portal are created automatically on your start date. Access to team-specific tools (GitHub, AWS, Salesforce, etc.) is requested by your manager through the IT Service Catalog.

## Asset acknowledgement
You must accept the asset handover in PeopleHub under **My Assets** within 3 days. The asset tag on the bottom of the laptop is used to identify your device in IT tickets.
